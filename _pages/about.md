Welcome! I am a PhD student in Economics at [The Ohio State University](https://economics.osu.edu/). My research lies at the intersection of **macroeconomics, housing, household finance, and wealth inequality**.

My research focuses on household heterogeneity, balance sheets, and distributional macroeconomics. My current work studies how differences in homeownership, leverage, and portfolio composition shape households' exposure to local housing market risk and the distribution of wealth. More broadly, I am interested in how household heterogeneity affects the transmission and distributional consequences of macroeconomic policy.
